The UK's leading student rocketry and CanSat competition, ending in a launch week at Machrihanish Airbase in Scotland. Teams design, build and launch their own rocket to a target altitude of 2 km, carrying a CanSat (a satellite the size of a drinks can) that collects data during the descent.

Built in collaboration with Space Soc's rocketry division.
