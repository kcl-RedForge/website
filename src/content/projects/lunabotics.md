A year-long challenge, modelled on NASA's Lunabotics, to build a robot that works on the Moon. In a simulated lunar arena the robot must cross boulders and craters, dig lunar soil and build a protective berm, with extra points for doing it autonomously.

Sub-teams own whole subsystems: the digging mechanism, drivetrain, electronics or autonomy software.
