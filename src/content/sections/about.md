---
title: Built at King's
---

KCL RedForge is King's College London's elite student engineering team, gathering the most driven makers to compete in national and international competitions. It is created and sponsored by KCL Electronics, a community of around 130 makers of all levels, backgrounds and fields.

Last year KCL Electronics entered its first international competition, the European Rover Challenge, and began building a 75 kg Mars rover. This year RedForge is an application-based team, building for three competitions.
